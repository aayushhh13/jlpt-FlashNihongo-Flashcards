## ✨ What is it?

A single-page Japanese learning application featuring two dedicated flashcard modes. Designed for JLPT N5 and N4 learners, FlashNihongo makes vocabulary and Kanji practice simple, interactive, and engaging.

**No typing. No complicated quizzes. Just tap, flip, and learn.**

| | 🔵 Vocab practice | 🔴 Kanji practice |
|---|---|---|
| Cards | 1,430 words (N5 + N4) | 282 kanji (N5 + N4) |
| Front | Japanese word or meaning (your choice) | Big kanji or meaning (your choice) |
| Back | Meaning, reading, example sentence | Meaning, on-yomi, kun-yomi, example words |
| Theme | Blue | Light red |

Your progress is saved in your own browser, separately for vocabulary and Kanji.

## 🌟 Key Features

<details>
<summary><b>🔵 1. Vocabulary Learning</b></summary>

<br>

- Practice 1,430 Japanese vocabulary cards.
- Covers both JLPT N5 and N4 levels.
- Choose whether to display the Japanese word or its meaning first.
- Reveal meanings, readings, and example sentences.
- Listen to Japanese pronunciation using your device's speech synthesis.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔴 2. Kanji Learning</b></summary>

<br>

- Practice 282 Kanji characters.
- Organized into individual lessons.
- View meanings, on-yomi, and kun-yomi readings.
- Explore example vocabulary words.
- Choose individual lessons for focused practice.
- Show or hide example words according to your preference.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔄 3. Interactive Flashcards</b></summary>

<br>

- Tap or press a key to flip cards.
- Navigate forward and backward.
- Mark cards as **I know it** or **Still learning**.
- Shuffle cards or follow their original order.
- Study only the cards you haven't mastered yet.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💾 4. Automatic Progress Saving</b></summary>

<br>

- Progress is saved directly in your browser.
- Vocabulary and Kanji progress are maintained separately.
- Continue learning without manually saving your sessions.
- Reset progress whenever you want to start fresh.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📱 5. Mobile-Friendly Experience</b></summary>

<br>

- Designed for touch-based interaction.
- Swipe gestures for card navigation.
- Keyboard shortcuts for desktop users.
- Responsive interface for different screen sizes.

</details>

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Click **▶ Open the app** at the top.
2. Tap anywhere on the start screen.
3. Choose **Vocab practice** or **Kanji practice**.
4. Select your preferred learning options.
5. Tap the card to flip it.
6. Press **I know it** or **Still learning**.
7. Continue practicing and track your progress!

## 💾 Progress Tracking

FlashNihongo uses your browser's local storage to maintain your learning progress.

**How it works:**

- Your progress is stored locally on your device.
- Vocabulary and Kanji sessions maintain separate progress.
- Your saved progress remains available when you revisit the same website using the same browser and site address.
- No account or registration is required.

<details>
<summary><b>⚠️ Important things to know about saved progress</b></summary>

<br>

- Clearing browser storage or site data can erase your progress.
- Switching browsers or devices does not automatically transfer progress.
- Opening the application through a different URL may create a separate storage space.
- Reset progress only when you want to start over.

</details>

## ❓ Something is not working?

<details>
<summary><b>🔧 Click to view troubleshooting solutions</b></summary>

<br>

| Problem | Possible Solution |
|---|---|
| The page shows 404 | Check the website URL and deployment status. |
| I still see an old version | Hard refresh (`Ctrl + F5` on a computer). |
| My progress disappeared | Check whether browser data was cleared or the website address changed. |
| There is no sound | Speech uses your device's Japanese voice. If none is installed, the speaker button has nothing to play. |
| Cards are not flipping | Try refreshing the page or using `Space` / `Enter`. |
| Progress is not saving | Check whether local storage is enabled in your browser. |

</details>
